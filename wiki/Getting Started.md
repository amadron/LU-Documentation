# Getting Started

[Game Guide](./README.md) | [Worlds](./Worlds%20Guide.md) | [Quests](./Quest%20Guide.md) | [Items](./Item%20Guide.md)

New to LEGO Universe? Start with the missions in front of you, take time to
explore, and use your Passport to check what the game wants you to do next.
The original 2010 game guide describes the route below; individual servers
may change missions, controls, or available activities.

## Your first adventure

1. **Venture Explorer:** follow the crew's introductions to movement,
   interacting, building, and launching a rocket. Look around before leaving:
   the original guide treats the starting visit as a one-way trip. Do not
   assume the same restriction or achievement count applies on your server.
2. **Avant Gardens:** take missions at the encampment, practice combat and
   explore the Monument. Watch for equipment and activity tutorials.
3. **Nimbus Station:** meet the faction representatives and explore the hub.
   The [Faction Guide](./Faction%20Guide.md) helps you compare the choices.
4. **Pet Cove and beyond:** try the pet introduction, then explore Gnarled
   Forest and Forbidden Valley as their routes become available.

This is a historical starting route, not a mandatory order for every server.
For world-specific ideas and related entries, see the
[Worlds Guide](./Worlds%20Guide.md).

## Health, Armor, and Imagination

The original guide describes three resources worth watching:

- **Health** measures how much damage you can take before being smashed.
- **Armor** provides additional protection; equipment can affect it.
- **Imagination** powers activities and abilities, including some builds.

Look for pickups when your resources are low and read an item's effects
before equipping it. Some abilities require Imagination, so check the
on-screen feedback if one does not activate. Current values, equipment effects,
and recovery rules depend on the server and the specific item.

## Combat and rebuilding

Approach unfamiliar enemies carefully, keep moving, and compare the weapon
and ability descriptions in your inventory. Some weapons have successive
attacks or charged abilities; try them where there is room to retreat.
If you are smashed, use the offered rebuild prompt and check your health,
resources, and mission progress before trying again. The 2010 guide describes
coins dropping when smashed, but loss and recovery rules may differ now.

## Useful habits

- Read the current mission objective and confirm progress in your Passport.
- Search the [item database](./items/README.md) when you need a lead on gear.
- Ask other players for help when an activity supports groups.
- Treat controls shown by your client as authoritative; bindings may differ.
