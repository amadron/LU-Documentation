# Pet Guide

[Game Guide](./README.md) | [Quest Guide](./Quest%20Guide.md) | [Item Guide](./Item%20Guide.md)

Some LEGO Universe creatures can be tamed and kept as pets. Not every creature
with a pet-related data entry is tamable, and a pet listed in the database
may not be enabled on your server. Use the in-game instructions whenever they
are available.

## Trying to tame a pet

1. Find a creature identified as tamable in the [Pet Database](./pets/README.md).
   Its page may show a likely area, but a recorded map position is not proof
   that the creature is currently there.
2. Check the entry for any requirements, such as enough Imagination, a
   prerequisite, or a required item. Historical notes and local server data
   are labeled separately.
3. Start the taming interaction if the game offers it. The server data
   describes a timed building puzzle: use the valid pieces shown by the
   activity and finish before time runs out.
4. If taming succeeds, follow the naming prompt. The pet should then appear
   in your pet inventory.

Exact requirements, puzzle pieces, and time limits vary by pet. The pet's
database page is the place to check those details; the general steps above do
not replace the activity's on-screen instructions.

## What pets can help with

The 2010 game guide describes pets helping to uncover buried treasure and
activate certain pet bouncers. It also describes a pet introduction with
Coalessa in Pet Cove. Look for the related prompts and missions in game:
individual interactions and their availability depend on the server.
The local server snapshot contains pet-dig and pet-bouncer code, but this
does not establish that every historical location or interaction works.

For example, the guide places the Doberman in Avant Gardens and the Elephant
in Gnarled Forest; the latter historically needed a Bag of Peanuts. The
[Doberman](./pets/5635.md) and [Elephant](./pets/3050.md) pages provide
local puzzle data and separate historical location evidence. Verify current
spawns and requirements in game.

## If taming does not start or fails

- Check whether the creature is actually one of the confirmed tamable entries,
  rather than a test or unresolved template.
- Check the pet page for a listed Imagination requirement or scene condition.
- During the puzzle, use the pieces that the game marks as valid and finish
  within the timer.
- If the pet or interaction is missing, it may not be enabled in this server
  version. A static map record cannot confirm a live spawn.

- [Pet Database](./pets/README.md): pet pages, taming evidence, possible locations, and related items or quests.
- [Pet locations](./pets/locations/README.md): recorded map placements; these may be inactive or gated.

The detailed pet pages include technical component values for reference.
Those values are not all used by the game and should not be treated as live
success odds or proof that a pet is available.
