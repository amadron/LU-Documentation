# Pet Guide

[Game Guide](./README.md) | [Quest Guide](./Quest%20Guide.md) | [Item Guide](./Item%20Guide.md)

Some LEGO Universe creatures can be tamed and kept as pets. The client data
has 26 confirmed tamable pets. A few other pet entries are test objects or
unfinished records, and a pet listed in the database may not be enabled on
your server. Use the in-game instructions whenever they are available.

## Before you start: Pet Cove

Pet taming starts in **Pet Cove** with **Coalessa - Assembly Mystic**. If you
try to tame a pet before that, the client shows: *"This could be your pet!
Learn Pet Taming from Coalessa at Pet Cove."*

- [**Tame a Pet**](./quests/110.md) (Coalessa): tame a Bunny, Terrier, or Cat
  in Pet Cove. Coalessa's offer text says you use the Pet Emote to start the
  Pet Taming mini-game.
- [**Fetch!**](./quests/111.md) (Coalessa, after Tame a Pet): get Coalessa's
  Flying Disc back from the seagull. To do this you use your pet on a Pet
  Bouncer. The reward is the **Water Sprayer Module 1**.
- [**Lost Tags**](./quests/842.md) (Bella Pepper): find all 5 Dog Tags around
  Pet Cove. Bella then tells you about pet digging. The next mission,
  [**Can you dig it?**](./quests/843.md), asks you to have your pet dig up a
  buried treasure chest.
- Later Coalessa missions: **Master Pet Tamer** (tame 7 pets) and
  **King of Beasts** (tame 18 different pets).

You also need a free slot in your **Models** bag, because tamed pets are kept
there: *"Your Models bag is full. You'll need space in your Models bag before
you can tame a pet."*

## The taming mini-game

1. Walk up to a wild pet and interact with it. You need at least as much
   Imagination as that pet's puzzle costs (see the table below). If you don't
   have enough, the game says: *"You must find more Imagination to play the
   Pet Taming game."*
2. The puzzle shows a set of bricks. The client tutorial says: *"Which of these
   bricks belong in the model? Click them before your Imagination runs
   out!"* Some bricks belong in the model and some don't. Select the ones that
   belong and press **Build**.
3. **Each Build attempt costs the puzzle's Imagination cost.** If a wrong
   attempt leaves you with less Imagination than the cost, taming fails
   (*"You'll need more Imagination if you want to try taming this pet
   again."*).
4. The puzzle has a time limit of 30, 45, or 60 seconds, depending on the
   pet. If the timer runs out, the attempt fails.
5. If you succeed, the pet is yours. Name it (see [Naming your pet](#naming-your-pet)).

Two achievements reward fast taming: **Speed Tamer 1** (tame a pet in less
than 14 seconds) and **Speed Tamer** (less than 6 seconds).

Notes on the special requirements:

- **Bag of Peanuts:** The Elephant spawns use precondition 48 (have a
  [Bag of Peanuts](./items/6265.md)). Without it, the game says *"Elephants
  need peanuts before they can be tamed."*
- **Water spray:** On the server, the Skunk and both dragons are hostile. If
  you hit one with water spray, it can be tamed for 30 seconds, and it turns
  hostile again if you fail or quit. The mission **Make a Stink** says to
  *"Use your Water Sprayer on a Skunk and then tame it."*
- **Rockolate Bars:** The Earth Dragon checks precondition 279: *"You need 5
  Rockolate Bars to tame this pet."* (see [Rockolate Bar](./items/14511.md)).
- **Faction pets:** **Faction Pet Tamer** asks you to *"Tame your Faction
  pet in Nexus Tower."* The task texts name the four faction pets and where
  they are, as shown in the table. The Gryphon task places the Sentinel pet
  in Pet Cove, not in Nexus Tower.
- **Membership:** The Doberman and Buffalo map spawners use precondition 247.
  Its failure text is *"Become a LEGO Universe member to explore new
  worlds!"*

## Naming your pet

After a successful build, the client says *"Congratulations, you've
successfully tamed the pet! Now it is yours."* and asks you to *"Name your new
pet!"* Until you pick a name, the pet is called "*your name*'s Pet". The
name you type goes to moderation (*"Would you like to send … to the Mythrans
for approval?"*), and other players don't see it until it's approved. A
rejected name has to be replaced. On this server, a muted account's pet is
always named "Pet".

## Having a pet

- **One pet at a time.** All your pets are under the **Models** tab of your
  Backpack, but only one can be out. Taming a new pet puts the current one to
  sleep: *"Your pet just hibernated because you're taming a new one!"*
- **Pet commands:** The pet window has Come Here, Follow Me, Hibernate,
  Release, and emotes such as Bow, Howl, and Scratch Ear.
- **Imagination upkeep:** With the server option `pets_take_imagination=1`
  (the default in this setup), a pet costs 1 Imagination when you summon it
  and 1 more every 60 seconds. When your Imagination reaches 0, the pet
  hibernates.
- **Digging:** After you finish **Lost Tags**, your pet walks to a nearby pet
  dig (a treasure mound) and digs it up. Only the Skeleton Dragon can dig up
  the bone digs. Achievements such as **Pet Excavator** (dig up 6 different
  pet digs in Pet Cove) and the Ninjago Monastery pet-dig tasks use this.
  Some digs spawn things: in Gnarled Forest a dig spawns a Crab, on Crux Prime
  one spawns the Skeleton Dragon, and in Avant Gardens one spawns a Pet
  Bouncer.
- **Pet Bouncers:** *"A pet can activate this Pet Bouncer."* You unlock this
  through Coalessa's **Fetch!** mission. The client hint is *"Talk to
  Coalessa to unlock the Pet Bouncer ability."*
- **Pet switches:** Your pet walks over to unused pet switches nearby and
  presses them.

## If taming does not start or fails

- Did you start pet taming with Coalessa in Pet Cove?
- Is your Models bag full?
- Do you have enough Imagination for at least one attempt (see the table)?
- Does the pet need something first: peanuts, water spray, Rockolate Bars,
  the Panda foot race, a dig or rebuild, or your faction?
- Did you pick only the bricks that belong in the model, before time ran out?
- If the pet or interaction is missing, it may not be enabled in this server
  version. A map record doesn't prove the pet spawns there.

## Source and limitations

- **Client database** (`CDServer.sqlite`): `TamingBuildPuzzles` (Imagination
  per attempt, time limit, pieces), `PetComponent` (60-second Imagination
  drain rate), `Missions`/`MissionTasks` (pet-taming missions and
  achievements, which give the world for each pet), and `Preconditions` 48,
  247, and 279.
- **English client strings** (`locale.xml`): mission, object, item, and
  precondition names, plus the pet-taming UI texts (`PTMG_*`, `UI_PET_*`,
  `PR_*`) quoted above.
- **DarkflameServer source:** `PetComponent.cpp` (Imagination check and
  per-attempt charge, timer, naming and moderation, switches, digging after
  Lost Tags, Imagination drain), `PetDigServer.cpp` (special digs),
  `DamagingPets.cpp` (water spray), and `NjEarthPetServer` (Rockolate Bars).
  Other servers may behave differently.
- The Lion's location is historical only. No Lion spawn or location
  achievement was found in the local data.
- The Speed Tamer time limits (14 and 6 seconds, client field `taskParam1`)
  come from the client data. This server's mission code doesn't appear to
  check the time, so it may count any successful tame.
- Historical pet introduction, activities, and examples: user-provided
  *LEGO Universe Official Game Guide* (2010), pp. 40–41 and 44.
