# Summoner

[Classes](./README.md) | [Faction Guide](../Faction%20Guide.md) | [Engineer](./engineer.md) | [Inventor](./inventor.md)

**Faction:** Assembly · **Role:** Summoner / support · **Currency:** Assembly Token (LOT 8318)

A creature summoner. The staff's charge attack restores armor to nearby allies, and the hat, orb, and rank-3 shoulderpads summon a Bulper, a Thwok, and Scratch to fight alongside you.

## Unlocking the class

1. **Starter kit:** after joining Assembly, choose [Rank 1 Summoner Kit Package (LOT 8033)](../items/8033.md) as the reward of
   *Get your … Gear!* (offered by Nexus Jay). It holds the four rank-1 pieces and the
   rank-1 book. The book is also sold by your Nimbus Station vendor if you start a
   second class later.
2. **Use the class book** for each rank to earn the rank achievement the gear requires.
3. **Wear the full set** of a rank to earn the next rank's book.
4. **Buy the next rank** with faction tokens once you reach its level.

| Rank | Level | Required achievement | Class book | How you get the book |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 | *Assembly Summoner 1* | [Rank 1 Summoner Book (LOT 131)](../items/131.md) | Starter package; Nimbus Station vendor |
| 2 | 15 | *Assembly Summoner 2* | [Rank 2 Summoner Book (LOT 9970)](../items/9970.md) | Reward for wearing the full rank-1 set |
| 3 | 30 | *Assembly Summoner* | [Rank 3 Summoner Book (LOT 9971)](../items/9971.md) | Reward for wearing the full rank-2 set |

**Vendors:** ranks 1–2 at [Meldric Steamvalve - Assembly Vendor (LOT 6633)](../items/sources/object-6633.md) in Nimbus Station; rank 3, color
variants, and valiant gear at [Torbert Oscillator - Assembly Vendor (LOT 13380)](../items/sources/object-13380.md) in Nexus Tower.
