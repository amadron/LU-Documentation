# Racing quests and achievements

[Racing index](./README.md) | [Quest and achievement catalog](../quests/README.md)

This index reuses the existing quest database; it does not copy quest pages or rewards. It includes all local mission definitions with an attached racing task (task type 23), definitions already classified under the existing Racing passport category, and the locally identified Monument Race chain/medal definitions. Category labels and title associations are preserved as source classifications and are not independently proof of playable access.

- **Definitions with a racing/23 objective:** 84
- **Attached objective rows:** 84
- **Definitions in the existing Racing category:** 66
- **Unique linked pages in this index:** 97

Param values are interpreted using [eRacingTaskParam.h](https://github.com/DarkflameUniverse/DarkflameServer/blob/8b7d1b946b5e7df18f2c82ccabcb2634aefc11f4/dCommon/dEnums/eRacingTaskParam.h). Track IDs in type-23 task targets refer to world/instance IDs (unless that parameter changes the namespace); lap-time and total-time thresholds are stored in milliseconds. Parameter 4/5 targets may be mission IDs rather than world IDs, and parameter 17 targets smashable LOTs. The quest pages show the raw typed fields and exact local semantics; consult them for prerequisites and reward choices.

**Names:** quest and achievement names are the English client names (`Missions_<id>_name` in locale.xml). This replaced the earlier “Quest 624”/“Achievement 678” placeholders and corrected three earlier community-name guesses: 637 is “That Velocity Lass” (not “That Venture Lass”), 663 is “Twenty-Time Champion” (not “Fifty-Time Champion 1”; Fifty-Time Champion is 1774) and 1884 is “Faster and More Furious” (not “Monument Race (Mission)”). Some names repeat because the client reuses them, for example the daily “Vertigo Debris”, “Dragonmaw Debris”, “Drive Five” and “… Pro” missions, and series whose last step has no number (678 “Go Outside and Play” follows 675–677 “Go Outside and Play 1–3”). Track names come from the client zone and activity names. World 1261 is shown as “Space Blizzard Racetrack (1261)”: that name is the client's activity name for activity 60, and 1261 has no ZoneTable row (see [Space Blizzard](./tracks/space-blizzard.md)).
