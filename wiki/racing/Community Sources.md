# Public racing sources and historical context

[Racing index](./README.md) | [Methodology](./Methodology.md)

Read-only research was done through the public Fandom MediaWiki API. Each row points to the exact article revision used and the date retrieved (2026-11-17). Facts from these pages are summarized here and on the relevant detail pages; no walkthrough prose or large tables are copied. The public wiki documents historical LEGO Universe content and is not evidence that the checked DarkflameServer snapshot currently enables it.

Historical mission prerequisite/reward statements are linked back to the existing quest pages instead of being assigned to local IDs by title alone.

Direct source URLs follow the MediaWiki revision format `/wiki/<title>?oldid=<revision>`. API pages were retrieved from `https://legouniverse.fandom.com/api.php`; normal HTML pages were not required. Public source material was not joined to an item/quest ID solely by a similar name.
