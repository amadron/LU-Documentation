# NPCs and Enemies

[Game Guide](../README.md) | [Quest NPC index](../quests/NPC%20Index.md) | [Pet database](../pets/README.md) | [Worlds, zones, and maps](../zones/README.md)

**1,403 NPC, enemy, and user-created NPC templates have individual pages by LOT.** The catalog uses the local NPC template list; entries are definitions, not proof that a character is active or available in a particular server.

Coordinate-backed placements are available for 52 templates in the current world-marker dataset. Their coordinates identify potential spawns or placements, not live entities.

Indexes contain at most 200 templates each. Pages report available classification, registered components, AI/creature fields, mission associations, and mapped placements without inferring missing values.

The local catalog also contains component-only registrations and other non-actor object types; those records are not included in this explicit NPC/enemy catalog. Pet-focused details are in the [Pet database](../pets/README.md).
