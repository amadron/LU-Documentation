# Worlds Guide

Each world has its own missions, people, and activities. The descriptions
below use the 2010 guide as historical orientation, not as evidence that
every entrance, mission, or activity works on your server. Use your in-game
map and journal for the current route.

| Place | What to look for | Local reference |
|---|---|---|
| Venture Explorer | The opening tutorials, crew missions, and your first rocket. Explore before leaving; the original guide describes the first visit as one-way. | [Quest locations](./quests/locations/zone-1000.md) |
| Avant Gardens | Early combat, the Sentinel encampment, the Monument, and introductory activities. | [Quest locations](./quests/locations/zone-1100.md) |
| Nimbus Station | A central hub with faction representatives, vendors, launch points, and a race area. | [Quest locations](./quests/locations/zone-1200.md) |
| Pet Cove | Pet-related introductions and missions; look for Coalessa. | [Quest locations](./quests/locations/zone-1201.md) |
| Gnarled Forest | Pirate-themed areas, pets, a shooting activity, and a raceway. | [Quest locations](./quests/locations/zone-1300.md) |
| Forbidden Valley | Ninja-themed missions, difficult terrain, pets, and dragon-related activities. | [Quest locations](./quests/locations/zone-1400.md) |
| Starbase 3001 | A smaller historical side destination accessed from Nimbus Station. | [Quest locations](./quests/locations/zone-1600.md) |

The [complete location index](./quests/locations/README.md) includes additional
areas from the local data, including worlds not covered by the 2010 guide.
It lists candidate associations rather than confirmed live spawns.

## Explore without missing a lead

1. On arrival, use the map and speak to available mission characters.
2. Track the exact objective before traveling; some tasks need a specific
   interaction, item, or result.
3. Check side paths for activities and collectibles, but use the journal to
   confirm credit.
4. Before traveling away, review unfinished tasks and any return restrictions
   shown by the game. Historical restrictions may no longer apply.

For activity-specific details, see the [Pet Guide](./Pet%20Guide.md),
[Racing Guide](./Racing%20Guide.md), and
[Building Guide](./Building%20Guide.md).

## Source and limitations

The original route and world descriptions are summarized from the
user-provided *LEGO Universe Official Game Guide* (2010), pp. 89–164.
The linked local indexes provide separate static-data evidence. Neither
source alone proves that an activity is available in a particular deployment.
