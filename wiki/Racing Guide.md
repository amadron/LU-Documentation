# Racing Guide

[Game Guide](./README.md) | [Quest Guide](./Quest%20Guide.md) | [Item Guide](./Item%20Guide.md)

Racing is one of the activities you can try while exploring LEGO Universe.
The local data describes several car tracks, timed courses, and racing-related
missions and achievements. Use the in-game activity or track entrance if it
is available on your server; stored track definitions do not guarantee that
every activity is currently enabled.

## Getting started

1. Find an available race entrance or activity in the world.
2. Check the in-game instructions and vehicle requirements before joining.
   The car tracks use modular racing vehicles; see the [race car reference](./racing/tracks/race-cars-and-modules.md)
   if you want to look up the local evidence.
3. Follow the course and its checkpoints, and watch the activity prompts for
   the goal. The number of laps and rules depend on the track.
4. Finish the race and check your result. Some missions ask for a finish,
   others for a placement, time, pickups, or a clean run without being smashed.

## Make progress on racing missions

Your mission journal tells you which race and result count. “Complete a race,”
“win,” and “finish under a time” are different goals; racing on any track may
not count for a track-specific objective.

The [racing mission index](./racing/Racing%20Quests%20and%20Achievements.md)
links to the individual quest and achievement pages. If a counter does not
move, check the exact track, result, and any conditions shown in your journal.

## Tracks and rewards

The [Racing Database](./racing/README.md) has pages for the known car tracks,
foot races, and related activities. Track pages include entry-location
evidence, locally recorded rules, and reward definitions.

Rewards can depend on your result and on how the server is configured. A
listed reward or modeled item chance is not a guarantee that every race awards
that item. Check the activity result and your inventory after finishing.

## If a race is unavailable

The track may be disabled, seasonal, or unavailable in the server version you
are playing. If you need help, tell staff the track name, zone, what you
interacted with, and what the game did instead.

The database contains technical source fields for readers who want them; they
are not required to take part in a race.
