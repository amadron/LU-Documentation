# Faction and Specialty Kit Guide

[Game Guide](./README.md) | [Getting Started](./Getting%20Started.md) | [Worlds](./Worlds%20Guide.md) | [Items](./Item%20Guide.md)

Factions give your Minifigure a theme and access to specialty equipment.
The original 2010 guide describes a choice in Nimbus Station after you meet
the four representatives. Compare the available kits and read the in-game
confirmation before choosing: the historical guide describes faction choice
as permanent. Whether a server offers a way to change it is server-specific.

## Compare the original choices

| Faction | Specialty kits in the 2010 guide | General theme |
|---|---|---|
| Assembly | Engineer, Summoner | Building, support, and summoned helpers. |
| Paradox | Sorcerer, Space Marauder | Maelstrom-inspired abilities and ranged attacks. |
| Sentinels | Knight, Samurai | Combat and protection. |
| Venture League | Buccaneer, Daredevil | Exploration, mobility, and adventurous fighting styles. |

These are broad descriptions, not a promise that every historical ability is
implemented. Abilities are tied to equipment and rank; inspect the actual
gear and its requirements before spending currency or selecting a kit.

## Historical introduction at Nimbus Station

The 2010 guide describes Nexus Jay sending players to meet the representatives
and earn four badges before returning to choose:

- **Assembly:** help Mardolf with a rocket-building assignment.
- **Paradox:** collect Maelstrom Bricks for Silas Penumbra.
- **Sentinels:** complete a survival challenge for Melodie Foxtrot.
- **Venture League:** help Johnny Thunder find treasure chests.

Names and objectives are useful starting clues, but the live mission journal
is authoritative. See the [Quest Guide](./Quest%20Guide.md) and
[quest database](./quests/README.md) to check a particular objective.

## Choosing and progressing

1. Decide whether the faction's theme and available kit equipment appeal to you.
2. Compare the abilities actually shown in your client, including any costs.
3. Confirm the choice only after reading any permanence warning.
4. As you progress, check faction missions and vendors for equipment with
   different requirements and abilities.

An item or ability listed in a reference may be unavailable, modified, or
incomplete on your server. See the [Item Guide](./Item%20Guide.md) for
equipment and vendor checks.

## Source and limitations

Historical faction names, introductory assignments, and kit themes are
summarized from the user-provided *LEGO Universe Official Game Guide* (2010),
pp. 9–10 and 22–39. Later game content and DarkflameServer behavior may differ.
